Looking for BBQ catering in Bali? We bring a full barbecue and a private chef straight to your villa, garden or beach house — anywhere on the island. You choose the menu and the guest count; we arrive with the BBQ, the charcoal and gas, fresh food bought that morning, and a chef who grills everything, serves it buffet-style and cleans up before leaving.

It's the easiest way to feed a group in Bali. No booking a restaurant table for twenty, no taxis across town, no cooking or washing up — just a fresh, flame-grilled feast where you're already staying. We cater everything from a 6-person villa dinner to 200+ guest weddings, with per-person pricing that gets better as your group grows.

How our BBQ catering works

  1. Pick your menu & price. Choose a package or build your own on the menu & prices page, or a budget option from the home page.
  2. Send it on WhatsApp. Your order and total pre-fill a message. We confirm your date, details and final price.
  3. Secure the date. A 50% deposit holds your booking; the balance is paid on the day.
  4. We fire up the grill. Your chef arrives, cooks a fresh feast and tidies away. Read more on how it works.

BBQ catering prices in Bali

Menus start from around $11 USD per person (about IDR 200,000), dropping as your group grows across the 6–9, 10–19 and 20+ guest tiers. A flat BBQ & chef hire fee of IDR 1,800,000 applies to every booking — discounted 20% for 10+ guests and 50% for 20+, and at 30+ guests we add a second BBQ and an extra chef. A delivery fee applies by area (from IDR 200,000). See exact totals live in IDR and USD on the menu & prices and budget pages.
